FAQ

Questions store owners ask us

Something else? Message us and a real person will reply.

Is Kahero BIR-accredited?

Kahero is built around BIR requirements: the VAT breakdown, SC/PWD discounts, sequential invoices and X/Z readings. Accreditation is done per business and per machine. On the Kadena plan we handle the paperwork with you, from the application to the Permit to Use.

What happens when the internet goes down?

The register shows a clear Online / Offline indicator so your cashier knows right away. Selling fully offline, with sales syncing once you reconnect, is on our roadmap. Until then we recommend a pocket Wi-Fi as a backup connection.

What hardware do I need?

Any recent tablet, laptop or PC with a browser. For a full counter, add a USB barcode scanner, a 58 mm or 80 mm thermal printer and a cash drawer. We can recommend local suppliers.

Can I accept GCash and Maya?

Yes. Record GCash, Maya, card and cash payments, or split one sale across several. The reference number is saved with each payment.

How are senior citizen and PWD discounts computed?

For qualifying items, VAT is removed first (price ÷ 1.12), then the 20% discount is applied, as RA 9994 and RA 10754 require. You choose which products qualify.

Is my data safe?

Your data is yours. Access is controlled by roles, every change is logged, and we handle customer data in line with the Data Privacy Act (RA 10173).
